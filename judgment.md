# Reusable physical rules

1. State the Hamiltonian terms and where nuclear repulsion is counted before comparing energies.
2. Check force signs at compressed and stretched configurations; a symmetric stationary point is an insufficient derivative test.
3. Small SCF residuals validate a numerical fixed point, not the state, basis, functional or scientific claim.
4. Entropy is state counting; attach an ensemble and standard state to every free energy.
5. Compare equal stoichiometry and area before taking a surface grand-potential envelope.
6. A trajectory with stable energy can still have inadequate sampling; timestep and event time are different quantities.
7. A NEB path needs metastable endpoints and refinement of its highest image before a saddle claim.
8. A barrier becomes a timescale only at specified temperature, prefactor and state population.
9. Converge a difference such as adsorption energy with consistent references, not an isolated total energy.
10. Bias-window overlap does not certify mixing in hidden coordinates; compare independent initial states.
11. Preserve detailed balance while perturbing kinetics; the highest isolated barrier need not control the net rate.
12. Separate SCF, geometry, numerical, state, model and claim validation. Choose the next calculation by which assumption it can falsify.
